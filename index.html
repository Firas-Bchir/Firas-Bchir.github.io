Fidèle loyalty, reinvented.

Tunisia's loyalty app for cafés, restaurants and shops. Customers collect stamps and points by scanning a QR code and get real value back. It works offline, and every reward redemption is cryptographically signed, so rewards can't be forged. Business owners manage it all from a web dashboard, with Wafi, an AI advisor for merchants.
